Tattva PDF Tools

Android app · Updated 28 November 2026

Summary: your files never leave your phone. The app makes, merges, splits, unlocks and compresses PDFs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, send or share any personal data, and it has no internet permission.

What the app accesses

Only the PDFs, photos and scans you choose, through Android's file and photo pickers or when you share a file to the app. While you work on a file, the app keeps a private working copy, which is deleted when you finish or the app restarts. Your original files are never changed.

Passwords

A password you type to unlock a PDF is kept in memory only for that task. It is never saved, logged or sent anywhere. The app removes passwords you already know; it never guesses or cracks them.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as theme, default page size and default compress size.
  • History: the name, size, date and saved location of files you saved. Clear it any time in Settings → Clear history. The files themselves are not affected.

None of this leaves your device. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Scanning

The document scanner is Google's on-device scanner, provided by Google Play services. Its screens run inside Google Play services; the scanned pages are returned to the app and processed on your phone. Google's handling of that component is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Saving and sharing

Files you save go to Download/Tattva PDF or Pictures/Tattva PDF, or a place you choose. When you tap Share, the file is handed only to the app you pick. What happens after that is governed by that app.

Network access

The app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. This is checked automatically every time the app is built. It contains no ads or analytics.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional): show progress while a large file is processed.
  • Storage (Android 8–9 only): save files to Download and Pictures. Newer Android versions don't need it.

Tattva PDF Tools is not affiliated with UIDAI or any government body.

Tattva Image Compressor

Android app · Updated 27 November 2026

Summary: this app does not collect, store, send or share any personal data. Every photo is processed on your phone, and the app has no internet permission at all.

What the app accesses

  • Photos you choose. You pick images with Android's photo picker, or share them to the app. It can only read the images you select, and never modifies or deletes your originals.
  • Compressed images you save. When you tap Save, a compressed copy is written to your gallery in Pictures/Tattva Compressor.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as theme and default compression options.
  • History of images you saved: file name, sizes, dimensions, date and a link to the saved copy. Clear it any time in the app; your gallery is not affected.
  • Temporary files created while compressing, kept in private app storage and removed automatically within 24 hours.

None of this leaves your device. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Network access

The app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. This is checked automatically every time the app is built. Links you tap in the app (this page, or emailing support) open in your browser or email app.

Photo metadata

By default, compressed images have their metadata removed, including location (GPS), camera model and date. You can choose to keep it in Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional): show progress while compressing several images.
  • Storage (Android 8–9 only): save compressed images to your gallery. Newer Android versions don't need it.

Tattva Finance Calculator

Android app · Updated 27 November 2026

Summary: this app does not collect, store, send or share any personal data. Every calculation happens on your phone, and the app has no internet permission at all. We never see the numbers you enter.

What the app accesses

Only what you type into a calculator, such as a loan amount, salary or interest rate. The app requests no permissions: no contacts, photos, location, SMS or files.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as theme.
  • Last values in each calculator, so leaving a screen doesn't lose your work.
  • Saved calculations: the name you give them and their inputs. Delete them any time in the app (Saved → Delete), or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

None of this leaves your device. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Sharing a summary

When you tap Share, the app creates a summary image or text in its private storage and hands it only to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email). What happens after that is governed by the app you chose.

Network access

The app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. This is checked automatically every time the app is built. It contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party SDKs that collect data.

Calculations are not advice

Results are estimates for understanding only and are not financial or tax advice. Each result's Calculation Passport explains the method and assumptions used.

If this changes

If we ever add anonymous usage statistics, we will update this section and the app's Google Play data safety information before that version is released. The numbers you enter would never be included.

Children

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. When we release a new app, we add its own section here.
