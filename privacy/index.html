How Tattva Image Compressor handles your information, in plain language.

The short version

Tattva Image Compressor does not collect, store, send or share any personal data. Every photo is processed on your phone, and the app has no internet access at all.

No data collectedNo internet permissionNo ads or trackersOriginals untouched

What the app accesses

  • Photos you choose. You pick images with Android's photo picker, or share them to the app. It can only read the images you select, and it never modifies or deletes your originals.
  • Compressed images you save. When you tap Save, a compressed copy is written to your gallery in Pictures/Tattva Compressor.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as theme and default compression options.
  • History of images you saved: file name, sizes, dimensions, date and a link to the saved copy. Clear it any time in the app; your gallery is not affected.
  • Temporary files created while compressing, kept in private app storage and removed automatically within 24 hours.

None of this ever leaves your device. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Network access

The app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. This is checked automatically every time the app is built.

Photo metadata

By default, compressed images have their metadata removed, including location (GPS), camera model and date. You can choose to keep it in Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional): show progress while compressing several images.
  • Storage (Android 8–9 only): save compressed images to your gallery. Newer Android versions don't need it.

Children

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
