Privacy Policy

Tattva Image Compressor · Last updated: 27 November 2026

Tattva Image Compressor ("the app") is made by Tattva Digital Labs. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary: the app does not collect, store, send or share any personal data. All image processing happens on your device.

What the app accesses

  • Photos you choose. You pick images with Android's photo picker (or share them to the app). The app can only read the images you select. It never modifies or deletes your originals.
  • Compressed images you save. When you tap Save, the app writes the compressed copy to your gallery in Pictures/Tattva Compressor.

What the app stores on your device

  • Settings (theme, default compression options).
  • History of images you saved: file name, sizes, dimensions, date and a link to the saved copy in your gallery. You can clear it at any time in the app. Clearing history does not delete images from your gallery.
  • Temporary files while compressing, kept in the app's private storage and removed automatically within 24 hours.

None of this leaves your device. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Network access

The app does not have internet permission. It cannot send any data anywhere.

Photo metadata

By default, compressed images have their metadata removed, including location (GPS), camera model and date. You can choose to keep it in Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional): to show progress while compressing several images.
  • Storage (Android 8–9 only): to save compressed images to your gallery. Newer Android versions don't need it.

Children

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
