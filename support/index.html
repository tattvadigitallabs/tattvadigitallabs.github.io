Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. The app has no internet permission. Everything happens on your phone.
Are my original photos changed?
Never. The app only reads your photos and saves a separate compressed copy when you tap Save.
Where are compressed images saved?
In your gallery, in the Tattva Compressor album (Pictures/Tattva Compressor).
How does "compress to exact size" work?
Enter a limit like 200 KB. The app finds the best quality that fits. If a photo can't fit at good quality, it is made smaller in pixels instead of blurry. The result is always under your limit.
Why does an image say "Can't read"?
Some formats can't be read on every phone (for example HEIC photos on Android 8). Animated GIFs aren't supported.
Is location removed from photos?
Yes, by default. You can keep photo details (date, camera, location) in Settings.
