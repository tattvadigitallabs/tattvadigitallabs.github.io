Support

How can we help?

Quick answers to common questions, and a direct line to us if you need more.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The app has no internet permission, so it can't upload anything. Every step happens on your phone.

Are my original photos changed?

Never. The app only reads your photos and saves a separate compressed copy when you tap Save.

Where are compressed images saved?

In your gallery, in the Tattva Compressor album (Pictures/Tattva Compressor).

Why does an image say “Can't read”?

Some formats can't be read on every phone, for example HEIC photos on Android 8. Animated GIFs aren't supported.

Is location removed from my photos?

Yes, by default. You can choose to keep photo details (date, camera, location) in Settings.

Can I compress many photos at once?

Yes, up to 50 at a time. Compression keeps running in the background with a progress notification, and you can cancel any time.

Still need help?

Email us. Please include your phone model and Android version.
Tip: in the app, Settings → Contact support fills these in for you.
