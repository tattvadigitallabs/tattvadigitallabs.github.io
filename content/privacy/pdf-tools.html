Summary: your files never leave your phone. The app makes, merges, splits, unlocks and compresses PDFs entirely on your device. It does not collect, store, send or share any personal data, and it has no internet permission.

What the app accesses

Only the PDFs, photos and scans you choose, through Android's file and photo pickers or when you share a file to the app. While you work on a file, the app keeps a private working copy, which is deleted when you finish or the app restarts. Your original files are never changed.

Passwords

A password you type to unlock a PDF is kept in memory only for that task. It is never saved, logged or sent anywhere. The app removes passwords you already know; it never guesses or cracks them.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as theme, default page size and default compress size.
  • History: the name, size, date and saved location of files you saved. Clear it any time in Settings → Clear history. The files themselves are not affected.

None of this leaves your device. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Scanning

The document scanner is Google's on-device scanner, provided by Google Play services. Its screens run inside Google Play services; the scanned pages are returned to the app and processed on your phone. Google's handling of that component is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Saving and sharing

Files you save go to Download/Tattva PDF or Pictures/Tattva PDF, or a place you choose. When you tap Share, the file is handed only to the app you pick. What happens after that is governed by that app.

Network access

The app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. This is checked automatically every time the app is built. It contains no ads or analytics.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+, optional): show progress while a large file is processed.
  • Storage (Android 8–9 only): save files to Download and Pictures. Newer Android versions don't need it.

Tattva PDF Tools is not affiliated with UIDAI or any government body.
