Our principles

  • On-device first. Our apps process your content on your device wherever possible.
  • Collect nothing we don't need. If an app ever needs data to work, its section below will say what, why, and for how long.
  • No advertising or tracking. We don't include ad networks, analytics or tracking SDKs in our apps.
  • No selling or sharing. We never sell or share personal data.

This website
